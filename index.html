Problem Solving: Systems of Linear Equations

Lesson 7 - MATATAG Grade 8 Mathematics

1. The Core Objective

Students will solve problems involving systems of linear equations in two variables by modeling real-life situations. The goal is to identify two independent constraints that define a single solution.

Polya’s 4-Step Method (In Detail)

  • Understand: List given values. Identify what is missing (e.g., $x$ = price of apple, $y$ = price of orange).
  • Plan: Create two equations. Look for "Total" keywords.
  • Solve: Perform algebraic operations. If variables cancel out and $0=0$, there are infinite solutions. If $0=5$, there is no solution.
  • Check: Substitute values back into the *other* equation to verify.

2. Specialized Formulas

Different word problems require specific background knowledge:

  • Geometric: Supplementary angles ($A + B = 180^\circ$).
  • Interest: $Principal \times Rate = Interest$. If you have two accounts: $0.05x + 0.08y = Total Interest$.
  • Motion: $Distance = Rate \times Time$. In a river, your effective rate changes: $(Rate + Current)$ or $(Rate - Current)$.
